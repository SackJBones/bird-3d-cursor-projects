

I'm trying to design a VRChat world for to show off the VR 3D pointing technique Bird (Simonson & Gretton 2025, 
https://aubreysimonson.gitlab.io/page/projectPages/bird.html ). I'd like it to be grand in scale to reflect & exploit the essentially infinite range of Bird and feature organic forms similar to the shapes it's easy to draw in the air with Bird because it's so smooth and expressive, while still having a firm geometric flat-surfaces basis for ease of navigation--I'd like there to be variation in depth though, I think a conversation pit would be great, as well as a pool of water with fish in it and a big moat-like water-filled edge around part of one side, just before an overlook. As if Greg Lynn and Zaha Hadid teamed up to design a complex built into the side of a coastal mountain ridge (not a peak but a long, slightly curvy ridge running parallel to a shore like you might find in Barcelona or Sicily). I'd like a few disconnected but still easily navigated-between interior spaces that are cozy and fun to be in, each with one simple feature that makes it interesting and distinctive, with the main inspiration for layout and feel being the kitsunebi teahouse VRChat world--that said, I'd like almost everything to be white like much of Greg Lynn and Zaha Hadid's work, letting the forms be the main things, seeing as they're already pretty salient. this should work great for Quest users with a very high framerate, which might mean a rendering technique other than millions of small polygonal divisions to form the curves is called for, but I'm not sure what that would be--anyway we want stuff to be plain. we do need very large features visible over long distances from certain vantage points because Bird enables interaction at those ranges, so, no close culling plane, which will also constrain the computational load of the geometry. basically if the curves can be done parametrically so it's easy to reconfigure them with code and the rendering is lightweight by nature that would be perfect. could you please take a pass at generating an image of this world? please don't make it too realistic/dramatically shaded/detailed, it should look like about the level of rendering quality you can expect out of VRChat on Quest and feel familiar to VRChat users.
