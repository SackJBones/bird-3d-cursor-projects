# Cliff01 independent review

Review cycle 2026-09-29. Design authority remains Dana's complete preserved concept conversation and four WorldDesign20260927 references: a long coastal ridge, substantial but calm white architecture, useful social spaces, clear flat circulation and economical broad forms. The intervention is one local near-cliff refinement, preserving the authored complex, cavern/room envelope, routes and Bird behavior.

## First baked candidate: revise

**REVISE / do not retain as the final design.** I inspected [FirstCandidate](FirstCandidate) views `01` through `06`, baked around 12:51 UTC, and directly compared the matched [Before](Before) lookout-south view. These are fresh baked views; this assessment does not use the earlier mismatched-old-lightmap preview shading.

The changed middle band reads as a giant undercut or carved groove. Dark triangular pockets above the lookout in `01`, `02` and `04` suggest an overhanging cap instead of a shoulder that rises and recedes inland. There is more relief than the flat baseline, but the dominant impression remains a tall screen with a recessed trough. The pond approach (`03`) still faces a very large nearly planar wall.

The arrival control (`05`) appears materially preserved, and the cavity-side view (`06`) shows no obvious new interior intrusion from that free-camera angle. Those preservation findings do not make the exterior shape successful, and they are not an exhaustive cavity or closure test.

The proposed next revision should connect the new slope toward the unchanged crest, keeping the lower toe more seaward than the upper bands. Use a few unequal broad planes and gradual transitions at the patch ends. If the result still forms a conspicuous dark horizontal roofline, reduce the retreat amplitude; changing lighting or material brightness would not resolve the underlying shape. No fine rock noise, extra planting or additional architecture is needed for this correction.

## Second baked candidate: retain within scope

**RETAIN as a modest local improvement, not completion of the cliff design.** I inspected all seven final [Android views](Android), compared the first candidate, and directly compared the matched baseline lookout-south and complex-exterior views in [Before](Before).

The pronounced recessed roofline and dark triangular underhangs from the first candidate are gone in the two lookout views and the free exterior view (`01`, `02`, `04`). The larger planes now join the upper ridge more coherently. The unequal folds add some relief without introducing a detached rock, an extra peak or busy surface noise. This is a better direction for the long coastal ridge in the reference concept.

The improvement is limited. The pond approach and exterior (`03`, `04`) still face an enormous, nearly continuous wall; much of the changed relief is subtle at the scale of the complex. The existing cavern shoulder in the side view (`06`) remains a bulky separate-looking mass against that wall. The result has not yet achieved the reference's convincing relationship between inhabited architecture and a varied coastal landform. Removing the first candidate's new defect alone would not justify a higher whole-world score.

The arrival control (`05`) retains its circular opening, white interior and pickup framing without an obvious new rock intrusion. The water-return view (`07`) retains the visible target and pond through the same foreground rail gap. I see no material presentation regression from this bake in those controls. The free-camera exterior and cavity-side views explain the massing; they are not evidence that a visitor can occupy those camera locations, nor exhaustive enclosure checks.

The dominant limitations are still the broad cliff, stacked-platform massing, incomplete architectural occupation and uneven shading on some older surfaces; this small landscape correction does not resolve them.

**Best next bounded pass:** prototype one sheltered, visibly inhabited pocket at the inland side of the main elevated terrace. A restrained asymmetric white enclosure or back/roof transition, warm seating and one clear open outlook would give the large middle deck an architectural purpose beyond circulation. Keep the existing walking surface, stairs, tested beacon stances and main seaward view clear. Judge one editable prototype from the pond approach, the arrival reveal and an actual standing/seated view before spreading the treatment. This offers more potential improvement to the present stacked-platform impression than another cycle of minor edge finishing. The cliff remains a future massing task; fine rock noise would not solve its scale or breadth.
