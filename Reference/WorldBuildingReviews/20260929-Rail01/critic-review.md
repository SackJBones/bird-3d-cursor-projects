# Rail01 independent visual review

Reviewed 2026-09-29 by the independent world-design critic against Dana's expressed design intent: quiet, bright matte-white coastal architecture, legible routes, useful social places and ordinary editable assets.

## Bounded verdict

**PASS / retain the pond-and-coast rail intervention.** This is acceptance of the reviewed rail geometry and baked Android presentation, not acceptance of the whole world as finished.

I inspected all seven final Android captures, originally rendered around 06:50 UTC and preserved in [Android](Android), and directly compared the seated-water and east-walk views with [Before](Before). The earlier [unbaked candidate](Candidate01-Unbaked) established the changed terminal shape; final shading was judged from the fresh bake.

- The former alternating light/dark patches are materially gone from the changed rails. The long faces read as continuous, restrained matte white, particularly in `02-seated-water.png` and `06-east-walk.png`.
- The west terminal in `01`, `03` and `04` looks deliberately capped and supported. The slim end post resolves the previously unsupported horizontal tail without adding decorative bulk.
- I see no newly closed intended opening. The water and fish remain visible through the same gaps; the water beacon remains visible in `05-high-return.png`.
- The old segmented joins in the foreground lookout rail in `05` remain visible. That unchanged rail is outside this intervention and is not evidence that the new pond/coast sweep failed.
- `07-seaward-free.png` supports composition comparison, not a claim about a normal visitor viewpoint. The intervention does not materially alter the established architecture or vista.

No rail-specific blocking visual defect remains in these captures. Geometry, safety-boundary preservation and interaction regressions are covered by the parent agent's separate checks; this critique does not independently certify those tests.
