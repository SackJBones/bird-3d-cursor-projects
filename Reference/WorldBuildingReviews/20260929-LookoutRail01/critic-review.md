# LookoutRail01 independent review

Reviewed 2026-09-29 against the complete preserved concept conversation, the four WorldDesign20260927 images and the latest Label01 findings. The relevant intent is quiet matte-white architecture with broad, readable forms and useful distant Bird sightlines. This review covers only the high-lookout ellipse rail finish.

## Android verdict

**PASS / retain the bounded intervention.** No additional rail geometry or shading revision is warranted by these images.

I inspected all seven final baked [Android views](Android), rendered around 10:48 UTC, and directly compared [Before](Before) views `01`, `03`, `04`, `06` and `07`.

- **Supported return (`01`):** the wedge-shaped gap and abrupt per-segment face changes are gone. The top and side remain distinct, so the rail reads as a square section rather than a rounded tube. The water ring remains visible through the same opening below the rail; the new finish does not conceal it.
- **South and north loop (`02`, `05`):** the continuous line is quiet and bright. The retained post rhythm and open spans preserve the familiar outlook. There is no new decorative bulk.
- **Tight end and closure (`03`, `04`):** no conspicuous closure seam, sharp join defect or end bulge is visible. The posts look straight, joined to the top rail and supported at the slab. Broad light changes follow the surface rather than forming the former alternating blocks.
- **Overall lookout (`06`):** the ellipse and stairs retain their hierarchy. The rail reads as a consistent perimeter. Existing uneven shading on the lower terrace rim and nearby curved roof remains visible in both before and after; this is not a newly introduced defect.
- **Arrival control (`07`):** the circular threshold, pickup, support silhouette and label hierarchy remain materially consistent with the baseline. I see no material rebake regression in this sampled view.

Views `04` and `06` are explicitly free-camera inspection evidence. They do not establish normal visitor access to those camera positions. The return view establishes the visible gap in that capture, while the separate compiled interaction checks must establish whether the logical Bird segment still reaches the target.

The implementer reports 960 triangles, 1,440 closed oriented edges, 192 top-path rays, 48 posts and clear spans, the retained 0.13 m top width and byte-identical safety/slab assets. These records support the bounded nature of the change; they do not replace the visual findings above or establish physical safety/comfort by themselves.

## One next bounded recommendation

Prototype a **single near-cliff shoulder refinement** visible from the lookout and pond. The enormous flat near face in `02`, `05` and `06` now dominates these cleaner views. Use a few broad, asymmetrical rock planes to suggest a coherent coastal ridge, rather than small repetitive rocks or surface noise. Keep this to one editable static shoulder, outside the established room/cavern clearance and route envelope; preserve the tested enclosure and collision behavior. Compare ordinary lookout and pond views plus one exterior inspection before retaining it. This would address a larger design mismatch than extending rail polishing indefinitely, without requiring a full terrain or architectural redesign.
