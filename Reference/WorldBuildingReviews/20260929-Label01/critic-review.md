## Android verdict

**PASS / retain. No further label revision is warranted by these captures.**

I inspected all seven Android context views, rendered around 08:41 UTC and preserved in [Android](Android), and directly compared the east approach, rear view and arrival with [Before](Before).

- `01-east-approach.png`: the former dark mirrored tangle is now one readable title and instruction. Near-white lettering separates from the cliff without needing a large panel. The ring remains the primary target.
- `02-water-front-free.png` and `03-water-back-free.png`: each side presents one correctly oriented label. The wording remains the truthful targeting-preview instruction, "Point through to highlight". It does not promise enabled travel.
- `04-water-oblique-free.png` and `05-water-edge-free.png`: the label foreshortens naturally instead of revealing a second mirrored face. Edge-on text is not useful reading material, but that is acceptable for a fixed world-space sign; a billboard is unnecessary for this repair.
- `06-high-return.png`: the water ring remains recognizable through the same railing gap. The distant label is still too small to read at this capture scale, but it remains subordinate and does not cover the target or vista. This repair does not establish long-distance text readability.
- `07-arrival.png`: the brighter label remains secondary to the pickup and circular threshold. It is still small in the wide view; no claim of comfortable headset reading distance follows from this image.

The thin outline does not become a conspicuous graphic effect in the reviewed frames. There is no new opaque plate, rotating text, giant remote label or visual obstruction in the ring opening. The improvement fits the intended restraint of the world.

The implementer reports nine-angle sidedness comparisons and an opaque-blocker depth test. Those are separate technical evidence; the rendered context review independently establishes the visible improvement, not an exhaustive proof of all viewing angles.
